**Sep 11th, PowerUp 2026**: Prof. Hidalgo-Gonzalez and her students, Martin Staadecker, Paul Serna-Torre and Adam Sedlak,
gives a talk in the PowerUp Conference held in Colorado, Boulder. Martin presented Pyoframes, a python package for optimization; and Paul and Adam presented STING, an open-source tool for the modeling and simulation of electrical power systems. Prior to their presentation, professor Gabriela Hug and her student Desai Maitraya presented Hermes, a open-source Python package for 
the simulation of electromagnetic transient dynamics of power systems.
